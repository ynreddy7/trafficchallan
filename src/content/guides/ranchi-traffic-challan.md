<div class="answer-box">Ranchi has no city-only e-challan site — challans are checked and paid on Jharkhand Police's statewide portal, echallan.jhpolice.gov.in. For settlement, DLSA Ranchi (at Ranchi Civil Court) and JHALSA run the Lok Adalat route; towing and camera-enforcement details for Ranchi specifically are not officially published.</div>

Jharkhand has no separate state e-challan guide on this site yet, so this page covers both the general check-and-pay steps and what is Ranchi-specific: the portal, where a challan is settled, and what Jharkhand Police has and hasn't published about towing and cameras.

## Which portal actually handles a Ranchi challan?

echallan.jhpolice.gov.in is Jharkhand Police's own statewide eChallan portal — its homepage describes it as an initiative of Jharkhand Police, Government of Jharkhand, offering online penalty payment, cashless payment through POS terminals, violation-history tracking, downloadable digital challan records, and SMS notices to violators. It is reachable today and is the portal that actually transacts a Ranchi challan; there is no Ranchi-only payment site. Jharkhand's Transport Department site, transport.jharkhand.gov.in, separately links out to the central MoRTH NextGen portal (echallan.parivahan.nic.in) as a "Pay challan online" option, but that national portal could not be reached at the time of writing — use the Jharkhand Police portal below.

## How do I check and pay a Ranchi e-challan?

1. Go to echallan.jhpolice.gov.in and choose **Pay Challan** (Search Challan).
2. Enter your **vehicle registration number** and **challan number**, plus the captcha shown.
3. If a challan is found, pay it online through the portal's payment gateway, or in cash/card at a listed POS terminal.
4. For a portal issue rather than a dispute about the challan itself, email **echallan-support@jhpolice.gov.in**.

The portal does not ask for a DL number or an OTP to search — only the vehicle registration and challan numbers.

## Where is a Ranchi traffic challan settled — court or Lok Adalat?

Two official routes, both anchored in Ranchi itself:

- **DLSA Ranchi**, based at **Ranchi Civil Court** — chairman phone 0651-2202600 / 2202414 / 2280613, secretary mobile 9905334568, email dlsaranchi@gmail.com — is the District Legal Services Authority that takes up compoundable traffic challans at National Lok Adalat sittings.
- **JHALSA** (Jharkhand State Legal Services Authority), headquartered at Nyaya Sadan, Near AG Office, Doranda, Ranchi – 834002 (phone 0651-3510941, email jhalsaranchi@gmail.com), organises the sittings across the state. Its own event listing names 12 September 2026 as the date of its "3rd National Lok Adalat" (inaugurated at Dhanbad) — matching the national sitting calendar of 14 March, 9 May, 12 September and 12 December 2026, with no Jharkhand-specific postponement of the kind Karnataka and Delhi had this year.

Only compoundable offences are taken up at a Lok Adalat, and the bench decides each case individually — see [e-Challan Lok Adalat](/e-challan-lok-adalat/) for how the sitting itself works. A challan that is not compoundable, or not settled at a sitting, is heard before the jurisdictional magistrate's court in the normal way; neither jhpolice.gov.in nor JHALSA publishes a separate online "Virtual Court" for Jharkhand traffic challans.

## What's officially published about towing in Ranchi?

Nothing could be confirmed. Jharkhand Police's main site and the eChallan portal do not publish a towing policy, a tow-crane procedure, or a list of pound/yard addresses for Ranchi. Until that changes, the reliable route for a towed vehicle is to call the Jharkhand Police DG Control Room, **0651-2446607**, or dial **100** or **112**.

## Does Jharkhand Police publish Ranchi's camera-enforcement zones?

No. There is no published list of camera-monitored junctions or roads in Ranchi, and no public breakdown of camera-issued versus on-the-spot challans. If you want to see the evidence behind a specific challan, check its Challan Print after searching it on echallan.jhpolice.gov.in — that is where a photo, if one exists, would appear, not a public zone map.
