<div class="answer-box">Jaipur has no city-only e-challan portal — it uses the same statewide NextGen eChallan portal as the rest of Rajasthan. What is Jaipur-specific is where things happen locally: Rajasthan's Lok Adalat authority (RSLSA) is headquartered in the city, while a challan sent to court goes through the single statewide Virtual Court, not a Jaipur-only one.</div>

For the state-wide check and pay steps, official portals, notified fine amounts and dispute process, see the [Rajasthan e-Challan guide](/rajasthan-e-challan/). This page covers only what is different for Jaipur: which site actually handles the city's challans, where a court or Lok Adalat challan is heard, and what is — and is not — officially published for towing, cameras and local contacts.

## Which portal actually handles a Jaipur challan?

There is no Jaipur-only payment site. Jaipur Traffic Police challans flow into the same statewide NextGen eChallan portal, [echallan.parivahan.nic.in](https://echallan.parivahan.nic.in/challan/challan-services), that serves every Rajasthan city — the same portal the [Rajasthan e-Challan guide](/rajasthan-e-challan/) covers in full for check and pay steps.

Jaipur Traffic Police does maintain its own web addresses — jaipurpolice.rajasthan.gov.in and a dedicated jaipurtrafficpolice.rajasthan.gov.in — but neither could be reached to verify their content as of this page's last check: the first returned a server error and the second failed to resolve at all. Until one of them can be verified, this page relies only on the statewide portal and other Rajasthan government sites that did respond, and readers should be cautious of any third-party site presenting itself as Jaipur's "official" challan portal.

## Where is a Jaipur traffic challan heard — court or Lok Adalat?

Two separate routes exist, and neither is Jaipur-only in the way some other states' city courts are:

- **The statewide Virtual Court.** Rajasthan's listing on [vcourts.gov.in/virtualcourt/](https://vcourts.gov.in/virtualcourt/) carries exactly one traffic entry for the whole state — "Rajasthan (Traffic Department)" — with no separate Jaipur establishment. A Jaipur challan referred online falls under that same single entry that serves every other Rajasthan city; search it by Challan/Vehicle No., verify by OTP, and choose to pay or contest, as described for the whole state in the [Rajasthan e-Challan guide](/rajasthan-e-challan/).
- **National Lok Adalat, run by RSLSA.** The Rajasthan State Legal Services Authority — the body that organises Lok Adalat sittings across the state — is itself headquartered in Jaipur, at Vidhik Sewa Sadan, Vidhik Sewa Marg, Jaipur (tel. +91-141-2227481, fax +91-141-2227602, helpline 9928900900, toll-free 15100, email rj-slsa@nic.in). RSLSA's own site also publishes a "Cause List – National Lok Adalat (Jaipur)" separately from its Jodhpur list, confirming Jaipur is tracked as its own sitting rather than folded into a regional one. Only compoundable offences can be taken up at a Lok Adalat sitting, and outcomes are decided case by case — see [e-Challan Lok Adalat](/e-challan-lok-adalat/) for how the process itself works.

## What's officially published about a towed vehicle in Jaipur?

Nothing could be confirmed as of this page's last verification. The sites that would carry Jaipur's own towing or tow-yard process — jaipurpolice.rajasthan.gov.in and jaipurtrafficpolice.rajasthan.gov.in — did not respond when checked (one returned a server error, the other failed to resolve). No towing information appears on the statewide NextGen portal or on the Rajasthan Home Department's site either. Until an official Jaipur page can be reached and verified, the reliable route is to call the statewide traffic police helpline below, or dial 100 for Rajasthan Police.

## Does Jaipur publish e-challan camera enforcement zones?

No camera-zone or junction list for Jaipur is published on any official source that could be reached for this page. As with the rest of Rajasthan, enforcement runs through a mix of camera-based and on-the-spot challans, but neither a coverage map nor a list of monitored junctions is available publicly. If you want to know why a specific challan was issued, the Challan Print on the NextGen payment portal — not a public list — is what shows the location and evidence for that challan.

## Jaipur contacts

- **Traffic Police helpline**: 1095 — published by the Rajasthan Home Department as the statewide traffic police number; no separate Jaipur-only traffic line is published on a reachable official source.
- **Rajasthan Police (general emergency)**: 100 / 112.
- **RSLSA (Lok Adalat queries)**: tel. +91-141-2227481, helpline 9928900900, toll-free 15100, email rj-slsa@nic.in — Vidhik Sewa Sadan, Vidhik Sewa Marg, Jaipur.

For everything else — checking a challan, paying it, notified fine amounts, and disputing one — go to the [Rajasthan e-Challan guide](/rajasthan-e-challan/), the [fines list](/fines/), or [disputing a wrong e-challan](/wrong-e-challan-dispute/).
